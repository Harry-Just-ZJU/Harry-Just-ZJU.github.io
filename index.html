Research Interests

Lymphangioleiomyomatosis (LAM): Investigating genes or molecular mechanisms capable of compensating for or regulating the loss of TSC1/TSC2 that leads to mTORC1 suppression.

mTORC1 Regulation: Exploring additional genes beyond TSC that can selectively downregulate mTORC1 activity, functioning analogously to bi-steric mTORC1 inhibitors.

Immune Checkpoint Research: Studying immune checkpoint molecules overexpressed in LAM and related tumors, investigating genes capable of repressing immune checkpoint expression.

Gene Signature Identification: Identifying stable, small-scale gene signatures across platforms (microarray and RNA-seq) for LAM classification or subtype stratification.
